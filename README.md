# Sea Freight Cheat Sheet 🚢

A compact reference for everyday sea freight work: Incoterms® 2020, FCL vs LCL, container types, shipping documents, common surcharges and the abbreviations you meet on a freight forwarding desk.

> **Quick reference only.** Always check the official ICC Incoterms® 2020 rules, your sales contract and the carrier's current tariff before you quote or ship.

## Incoterms® 2020 at a glance

### Rules for any mode of transport

| Rule | Name | Risk passes to the buyer… | Main carriage paid by | Insurance obligation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **EXW** | Ex Works | when the goods are made available at the seller's premises, not loaded | Buyer | None |
| **FCA** | Free Carrier | when the goods are handed to the buyer's carrier at the named place | Buyer | None |
| **CPT** | Carriage Paid To | when the goods are handed to the first carrier | Seller | None |
| **CIP** | Carriage and Insurance Paid To | when the goods are handed to the first carrier | Seller | Seller, at least ICC (A) cover |
| **DAP** | Delivered at Place | at the named destination, ready for unloading | Seller | None |
| **DPU** | Delivered at Place Unloaded | at the named destination, once unloaded | Seller | None |
| **DDP** | Delivered Duty Paid | at the named destination, cleared for import, ready for unloading | Seller | None |

### Rules for sea and inland waterway only

| Rule | Name | Risk passes to the buyer… | Main carriage paid by | Insurance obligation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **FAS** | Free Alongside Ship | when the goods are placed alongside the vessel at the port of shipment | Buyer | None |
| **FOB** | Free On Board | when the goods are on board the vessel at the port of shipment | Buyer | None |
| **CFR** | Cost and Freight | when the goods are on board the vessel at the port of shipment | Seller | None |
| **CIF** | Cost, Insurance and Freight | when the goods are on board the vessel at the port of shipment | Seller | Seller, at least ICC (C) cover |

### Points that cause the most mistakes

- **Customs:** the seller handles export clearance in every rule except EXW; the buyer handles import clearance and duties in every rule except DDP.
- **C-rules split cost and risk:** the seller pays the freight to the destination, but the risk already passes at origin.
- **Containers:** when a container is handed over at a terminal rather than loaded on board by the seller, FCA, CPT or CIP fit better than FOB, CFR or CIF.
- **DPU** replaced DAT in the 2020 edition and is the only rule where the seller unloads at destination.
- **Name the place precisely** and the edition, e.g. *"CFR Nhava Sheva, Incoterms® 2020"*.
- "No insurance obligation" does not mean "no insurance needed": whoever carries the risk usually wants cover.

## FCL vs LCL

| | FCL – Full Container Load | LCL – Less than Container Load |
|---|---|---|
| **What it is** | One shipper uses (and pays for) the whole container | Several shippers share one container |
| **Pricing** | Per container | Per freight ton (W/M): per cbm or per 1,000 kg, whichever is higher |
| **Handling** | Packed at origin, opened at the final place of delivery | Consolidated at a CFS at origin, deconsolidated at a CFS at destination |
| **Pros** | Faster, less handling, lower damage risk | Pay only for the space you use |
| **Cons** | You pay for the full box even if half-empty | More handling, extra CFS charges, often longer transit |

## Container types

Approximate internal sizes. Exact figures and maximum payload differ by carrier and container, so always check the line's spec sheet.

| Type | Common codes | Internal L × W × H (approx.) | Volume (approx.) | Typical use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 20' Standard | 20GP / 20DV / 22G1 | 5.9 × 2.35 × 2.39 m | 33 cbm | Heavy, dense cargo |
| 40' Standard | 40GP / 40DV / 42G1 | 12.0 × 2.35 × 2.39 m | 67 cbm | General cargo |
| 40' High Cube | 40HC / 45G1 | 12.0 × 2.35 × 2.69 m | 76 cbm | Light, bulky cargo |
| Reefer | 20RF / 40RH | slightly smaller than standard | — | Chilled or frozen cargo |
| Open Top | 20OT / 40OT | as standard, no fixed roof | — | Tall cargo loaded from above, e.g. machinery |
| Flat Rack | 20FR / 40FR | no side walls or roof | — | Oversized and heavy-lift cargo |

**TEU** = twenty-foot equivalent unit (one 20' box). A 40' container counts as 2 TEU (1 FEU).

## Key documents

| Document | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Commercial invoice** | Shows the sale, value and terms; the basis for customs value |
| **Packing list** | Packages, marks, weights and dimensions |
| **Booking confirmation** | Carrier confirms space, vessel/voyage and cut-off times |
| **Export declaration / EAD** | In the EU, the Export Accompanying Document (German: *Ausfuhrbegleitdokument, ABD*) shows the goods are declared for export |
| **Bill of Lading (B/L)** | Receipt for the cargo and evidence of the contract of carriage; an original B/L issued "to order" is also a document of title |
| **Master B/L (MBL)** | Issued by the shipping line, usually to the forwarder |
| **House B/L (HBL)** | Issued by the forwarder or NVOCC to the actual shipper |
| **Sea waybill** | Non-negotiable; the cargo is released to the named consignee without original documents |
| **Telex release** | Original B/Ls are surrendered at origin so the cargo can be released at destination without them |
| **Certificate of origin** | Proves where the goods were made; can qualify for lower duties under trade agreements |
| **Arrival notice** | Tells the consignee and notify party the cargo is arriving and what is due |
| **Delivery order (D/O)** | Authorises collection of the cargo at the destination terminal |

## Common surcharges and charges

| Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **O/F** | Ocean freight – the basic rate |
| **BAF** | Bunker Adjustment Factor – fuel cost surcharge |
| **LSS** | Low Sulphur Surcharge – cost of compliant low-sulphur fuel |
| **ETS** | EU Emissions Trading System surcharge – carbon cost on voyages to, from and within the EU |
| **CAF** | Currency Adjustment Factor – exchange-rate surcharge |
| **THC** | Terminal Handling Charge – moving the container at the terminal (OTHC at origin, DTHC at destination) |
| **ISPS** | Port security surcharge |
| **PSS** | Peak Season Surcharge |
| **GRI** | General Rate Increase |
| **Doc fee** | Issuing the B/L and other documents |
| **VGM fee** | Submitting the verified gross mass |

## Demurrage vs detention

| | Demurrage | Detention |
|---|---|---|
| **Where the container is** | Inside the terminal | Outside the terminal |
| **What triggers it** | A full container stays at the port beyond the free days, e.g. an import not picked up in time | The carrier's container is kept beyond the free days, e.g. at the consignee's warehouse for unloading |
| **Who charges it** | Shipping line (sometimes plus terminal storage) | Shipping line |

Many carriers combine both into one free-time allowance, often called **D&D**. Free days and daily rates depend on the carrier, the port and your contract, so negotiate them before the cargo moves.

## Useful abbreviations

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **ETD / ETA** | Estimated time of departure / arrival |
| **ATD / ATA** | Actual time of departure / arrival |
| **POL / POD** | Port of loading / port of discharge |
| **CY** | Container yard – where full containers are received and stored |
| **CFS** | Container freight station – where LCL cargo is consolidated and deconsolidated |
| **VGM** | Verified gross mass – under SOLAS, the shipper must declare the weight of a packed container before it is loaded |
| **Cut-off** | Latest time for cargo, documents or VGM to reach the carrier for a sailing |
| **Roll-over** | Container moved to a later vessel than booked |
| **Transshipment** | Container moved from one vessel to another on the way |
| **NVOCC** | Non-vessel operating common carrier – issues its own B/Ls without operating ships |
| **Shipper / Consignee / Notify party** | Sender / receiver / party to inform on arrival |
| **W/M** | Weight or measure, whichever gives the higher charge (LCL) |
